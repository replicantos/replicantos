# Code of Conduct

ReplicantOS is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community.

Be considerate, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and focus
disagreement on ideas and evidence. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, sexualized attention, doxxing, or publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and made without retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to `admin@replicantos.com`. Include relevant
links or context. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as
practical.
